# Especificación de Redline Rider

Sintetizada por 9 agentes de investigación sobre las convenciones del género
(racer de moto en primera persona sobre autopista). Juego ORIGINAL: se replican
mecánicas, que no tienen copyright, no arte ni marcas de ningún título comercial.

## Concepto

**APEX LANE** — a first-person motorcycle highway racer for the browser, built on three.js + WebAudio, targeting 60 fps on a 2021 mid-tier phone and any desktop. You ride a helmet-cam sportbike down an infinitely streaming 4-lane highway, threading gaps in traffic at 100–320 km/h. Everything scores: distance, overtakes, and above all *close passes* — the closer and faster the pass, the bigger the payout, doubled again if you're riding the oncoming lane on a two-way road. Close passes also buy you time (+0.1 s each) in timed modes and charge a two-segment nitro bar, so risk is the only real economy.

Content is one road, re-skinned: 4 environments (Suburb, Desert, City, Winter) × 3 times of day (Noon, Sunset, Night) × 4 weather layers (Clear, Rain, Fog, Snow), assembled at runtime from a 40 m road chunk pool, one prop kit and one set-piece kit (tunnel / bridge / overpass). 3 bikes, 4 upgrade tracks × 5 tiers each, cosmetic paint. 45 career missions in 3 stages plus Endless, Time Trial and Free Ride. All audio is synthesised at runtime in WebAudio — an RPM×load engine model with a virtual 6-speed gearbox, speed-driven wind, spatialised traffic, procedural crash and a generated menu loop — so total download is dominated by 10 GLB models, 4 sky images and 2 texture atlases (~22 MB target, ~6 MB blocking tier).

No ads, no IAP: soft currency (Cash) and a small earned-only hard currency (Gold) both come from riding. Two taps from the menu to riding; under 2.0 s from crash to riding again.

## Modos

### Career

45 hand-authored missions in 3 stages of 15, strictly linear (mission N unlocks on clearing N-1). Each mission has exactly ONE primary objective plus at most one modifier ('and don't crash'), drawn from 7 objective types: REACH_DISTANCE (18 missions), OVERTAKE_COUNT (9), NEAR_MISS_COUNT (6), NO_CRASH_DISTANCE (5), REACH_TOP_SPEED (3), WHEELIE_TIME (2), SCORE_TARGET (2). Never the same type more than twice in a row; missions 1-3 are always REACH_DISTANCE with 1.35x timer slack (the tutorial ramp). Stage binds the world: S1 (1-15) Suburb / Noon / TWO-WAY, checkpoint bonus +7 s; S2 (16-30) Desert / Sunset / ONE-WAY, +6 s; S3 (31-45) City / Night, missions 31-40 ONE-WAY with Rain on 34/37/40, 41-45 TWO-WAY, +5 s. Timed missions carry a countdown seeded at time_limit_s = D/(targetAvgKmh/3.6) * slack, slack lerping 1.35 (m1) -> 1.05 (m45); from mission 18 on the raw timer alone is insufficient, so checkpoints (every 500 m) and close passes (+0.1 s) are mandatory. Fail on timer 0 or on crash (NO_CRASH types and any mission with the no-crash modifier fail instantly; other missions treat a crash as run-over, which usually fails the objective anyway). 3-star rating: 1 star = objective met, 2 = met with zero crashes, 3 = met, zero crashes, and margin (>=20% of timer remaining, or 1.25x a count target, or target speed +15 km/h). First clear pays reward_cash + reward_xp; replays pay 100% cash, 25% XP, no gold, no new stars unless improved. After 3 consecutive fails on the same mission a SKIP button appears at 15 + 5*(stage-1) Gold: unlocks the next mission, awards 0 cash and 0 stars, marks the mission 'skipped'.

### Endless

No timer, no objective. Run ends on the first crash. Player picks environment / time of day / weather / road type from the unlocked pool on the Ride Setup screen. Score accrues continuously per the scoring model with no cap on distance; cash = per-event cash * env multipliers. Unlocked after career mission 3. One CONTINUE per run, offered for 4 s after the crash: costs 5 Gold (or is free once per UTC day), respawns 100 m past the crash point at 60% of pre-crash speed with 3.0 s of invulnerability and a 3-2-1 countdown; combo resets to x1, cash and score are retained. Per-config personal bests tracked separately for one-way and two-way (best score, best distance, most overtakes, top speed, longest combo, longest wheelie).

### Time Trial

Timer-only mode, unlocked after career mission 8. Starts with 60.0 s. Checkpoint gates every 500 m grant +10 s; each qualifying close pass grants +0.1 s; a gap-thread grants +0.5 s. Crashing does NOT end the run: it costs 5 s and respawns the bike in lane 1 from a standstill after a 1.2 s reset, which is the real penalty. Run ends when the timer hits 0.0. Score and cash accrue as in Endless, with a x1.25 mode multiplier on run-total cash because the mode is short and dense. Records: best distance, longest run duration.

### Free Ride

Sandbox, always available from Mode Select. No timer, no objective, no score HUD (speedometer, gear and nitro only), no fail state — a crash respawns in place after 2.0 s with no penalty. Explicitly pays 0 Cash, 0 XP, 0 Gold so it cannot be used to grind. Any owned bike, any unlocked environment/time/weather, plus two sliders exposed only here: traffic density 0.0-1.0 and traffic direction. Used as the practice surface for control-scheme changes and tilt calibration; the pause menu's Controls panel is reachable without leaving the run.

## Pantallas (en orden de flujo)

### S00_SHELL — Pre-engine HTML shell

Painted from the initial HTML payload within 200 ms, before any three.js code parses. Inline critical CSS, background #07080C with a radial glow, meta theme-color matching, an inline SVG wheel-rim mark spinning via CSS transform only (swapped for an opacity pulse under prefers-reduced-motion), and the game wordmark as inline SVG. Runs the capability probe synchronously: WebGL2 -> WebGL1 -> unsupported; MAX_TEXTURE_SIZE; compressed-texture extensions (ASTC > ETC2 > BC > none) to choose the KTX2 transcode target; navigator.hardwareConcurrency, deviceMemory, devicePixelRatio; then a 300 ms offscreen 1024x1024 road-shader benchmark. Result maps to quality tier LOW/MED/HIGH (render scale 0.65 / 0.8 / 1.0). Loads the save blob from IndexedDB (localStorage mirror for schemaVersion + checksum), runs migrations, resolves locale. If flags.clean_shutdown is false, boots one tier lower and notes it.

**Transiciones:** Auto -> S01_LOADING once the engine module has parsed (shell DOM is destroyed only after the first real 3D frame is presented, with a 250 ms crossfade). Fatal probe result -> S15_ERROR.

### S01_LOADING — Loading (brand + progress + tips)

Full-bleed still (sky_day for a first boot, otherwise the destination environment's sky, dimmed 45% with a bottom vignette), wordmark top-centre, a byte-weighted progress bar (screen width minus 12%, 9 px tall, bright leading edge) with a tabular 0-100% numeral and a phase label (checking device / loading bike / loading road / preparing shaders / almost ready). One rotating hint from a 24-string pool, 4.0 s each with a 250 ms crossfade; the first hint on a first run is always the control hint. Mute toggle top-right. Progress rules: strictly monotonic, eases toward truth at max 140%/s, never static >1.5 s (creep capped at 95%), last 8% reserved for the shader warmup pass. Blocking TIER 0 = 6.0 MB transfer cap: UI/FX atlas, world atlas, one sky, equipped bike GLB, road kit, 3 traffic GLBs, string bundle. Warmup compiles every material variant via compileAsync, renders 3 throwaway 128x128 frames, and preallocates pools (24 traffic, 32 particles, 16 popups, 24 audio voices).

**Transiciones:** Auto -> S02_TAP when TIER 0 + warmup complete AND the two brand beats (0.9 s studio, 1.4 s logo, skippable by tap after 400 ms) have elapsed. Asset failure -> 3 retries at 400/1200/3600 ms -> S15_ERROR.

### S02_TAP — Tap to start (gesture gate)

Whole viewport is the button (also a real focusable <button> with an accessible label for keyboard/gamepad). Pulsing prompt low-centre: short label plus a tap glyph on touch or a key glyph on desktop, 0.9 s opacity/scale breathe. Ignores the first 300 ms. Inside the same gesture handler, in this exact order: AudioContext.resume() + play a 1-sample silent buffer (iOS unlock); requestFullscreen on the canvas container if touch or if the player previously chose fullscreen; screen.orientation.lock('landscape') in try/catch; start the procedural menu music fade-in and fire the ignition rev; request a wake lock if available.

**Transiciones:** -> S03_LANGUAGE on a first run with no supported locale match. -> S04_MENU otherwise (400 ms crossfade). Portrait touch device at any point -> S16_ROTATE.

### S03_LANGUAGE — Language + control scheme (first run only)

Two cards shown in sequence over the menu scene, total budget under 25 s. Card A LANGUAGE: only shown if navigator.languages produced no supported match; 5 locales listed in their own names (English, Espanol, Portugues (BR), Turkce, Deutsch), plus a units row (km/h default, mph auto-selected for en-US). A small globe button remains on card B so a wrong auto-pick is fixable. Card B CONTROLS: 4 options each with an 8-frame looping diagram — tilt, on-screen buttons (recommended default on touch), virtual handlebar, keyboard/gamepad (default on desktop) — plus a sensitivity slider defaulted to 1.0. Choosing tilt appends a calibration step: hold the device in your riding pose, tap to average 30 samples over 0.5 s as the neutral zero. Both cards write immediately to the save blob and set flags.firstRun = false. No name prompt, no notification prompt, no legal wall (no ads or third-party SDKs ship).

**Transiciones:** -> S04_MENU. Back is disabled; forward requires a choice.

### S15_ERROR — Boot failure / retry

One layout, seven handled cases, each with an icon, a one-sentence cause, one primary action and a copyable diagnostic code carrying the timing marks and probe results: NO_WEBGL, CONTEXT_LOST (auto-retries once after 1 s first), ASSET_FETCH_FAILED (non-critical assets downgrade instead of failing — a missing sky becomes a gradient dome), MANIFEST_MISMATCH (clears the hashed service-worker cache and hard-reloads once behind a guard flag), OUT_OF_MEMORY (drops one quality tier, clears TIER 1, retries once), STORAGE_BLOCKED (continues memory-only with a 'progress will not be saved' warning), TIMEOUT (>20 s in any boot state, offers 'skip optional content'). window.onerror and unhandledrejection funnel here during boot instead of dying silently.

**Transiciones:** RETRY re-enters the failed boot state; RELOAD reloads the page; STORAGE_BLOCKED continues to S02_TAP.

## Puntuación

**Two independent accumulators. Score is deliberately inflationary and drives leaderboards; Cash is flat-rate and drives the economy. Gold never drops from driving.**

MULTIPLIER: M = speedMult * wrongWayMult * comboMult, hard-capped at 60.
- speedMult = clamp(1 + (kmh - 100)/100, 1, 3) → x1.0 at 100, x2.0 at 200, x3.0 at 300; exactly 1.0 below 100 km/h.
- wrongWayMult = 2.0 while the bike's lane index is in the oncoming group AND kmh >= 60, else 1.0.
- comboMult = min(10, 1 + floor(comboCount / 3)).

SCORE EVENTS:
- Distance: 1 pt per metre, NOT multiplied (so parking in the oncoming lane cannot farm it).
- Plain overtake: 10 * M. Counted once per vehicle, latched, when the player's front plane crosses the vehicle's rear plane while closing. Oncoming vehicles count.
- Close pass (requires kmh >= 100), tiered by lateral clearance at the crossing frame: <= 1.20 m → 40 * M; <= 0.80 m → 100 * M; <= 0.45 m → 250 * M; simultaneous both-sides gap thread → 600 * M.
- Sustained speed tick, highest tier only (not additive): 20 pts/s at >= 100 km/h, 50 at >= 150, 100 at >= 200, all * M.
- Wheelie: 150 pts/s * M above 60 km/h, plus milestone bonuses 500 / 1,500 / 4,000 at 3 / 6 / 10 accumulated seconds. Milestones are only paid if the wheelie ends cleanly; ticks already awarded stand.
- Wrong-way tick: 30 pts/s * M.

COMBO: increments on close passes, wheelie milestones and each full second of wrong-way riding — NOT on plain overtakes or distance, or it would never decay in traffic. Window 2.0 s, reset to full on each qualifying event. Breaks on crash, on brake held > 0.5 s, on dropping below 100 km/h, or on window expiry. Each increment plays the next step of a semitone ladder (+0, +2, +4, +5, +7, +9, +11, +12, then hold) and punches the widget 1.35x for 150 ms.
Magnitude sanity check: at 250 km/h in the oncoming lane with a maxed combo a 0.45 m pass pays 250 * 2.5 * 2 * 10 = 12,500; a gap thread pays 30,000. Roughly one close pass per second sustains ~20M/hour, which is the right order for 8-digit endless scores.

CASH EVENTS (flat rates, explicitly WITHOUT speedMult and comboMult):
- Distance: 4 Cash per 100 m.
- Close pass by tier: 3 / 6 / 12 / 25 Cash (requires >= 100 km/h). Gap thread: 40.
- Sustained speed: 1 Cash/s at >= 100, 2 at >= 150, 4 at >= 200.
- Wheelie: 2 Cash/s. Wrong-way: x2 on every cash event while active.
- Plain overtakes pay ZERO cash — this is what pushes the player to take close passes above the threshold.
Run-total scalars applied once at the results screen: cashMult = 1 + 0.15 * (bikeTier - 1) → x1.00 / x1.15 / x1.30; env multiplier = product of Night x1.15, Rain x1.20, Fog x1.25, Snow x1.10, Two-Way x1.25, clamped at x1.60; mode multiplier Endless x1.00, Time Trial x1.25, Career x1.00 plus the mission's fixed reward, Free Ride x0.
Calibration: an aggressive two-way night lap on a maxed tier-3 bike lands at ~150 Cash per km, which is the figure the career reward curve and bike prices are balanced against.

TIME (timed modes only): checkpoint gate every 500 m grants +10 s in Time Trial, +7/+6/+5 s in career stages 1/2/3. Every qualifying close pass grants +0.1 s; a gap thread grants +0.5 s. Both fire a green flyout into the timer so the loop is teachable.

NITRO: 2 segments x 100 charge. Close pass grants 8 / 15 / 25 / 40 charge by tier; +3 charge/s above 150 km/h; a crash drains one full segment. Activating consumes one full segment: +35% thrust and a +8% max-speed overdrive for 2.5 s, with the needle allowed past the redline and the vignette punched +0.10.

FAILURE AND BANKING: a crash ends the run but never voids Cash already earned. A failed career mission pays 50% of driving-earned Cash and 0 mission reward, 0 XP. Free Ride pays nothing. XP = reward_xp on first clear, 25% on replay; in Endless and Time Trial XP = floor(score / 2000).

GOLD (earned only, no IAP): 1 Gold on every 5th career mission, 10 Gold on each stage final (missions 15 / 30 / 45), 25 Gold for all-3-stars on a whole stage, 1-3 Gold per achievement (24 achievements, ~60 Gold total), 2 Gold for completing all three daily quests. Spent on: mission skip (15/20/25), Endless continue (5), the 2 animated paint finishes (25 each).

## Progresión

**CAREER: 45 missions, 3 stages of 15, strictly linear.**
Cash reward: reward_cash(m) = round50(200 * 1.115^(m-1)) → m1 200, m5 310, m10 540, m15 940, m20 1,620, m25 2,800, m30 4,840, m35 8,360, m40 14,450, m45 24,960. Stage finals (15 / 30 / 45) pay x3. Total first-clear career Cash ≈ 231,000, plus roughly 30-50% of that again from driving-earned Cash on good runs.
XP: reward_xp(m) = 60 + 20*m → m1 80, m45 960. Player level curve: xpToNext(L) = 500 * L^1.35 (L1→L2 = 500, L10→L11 = 11,200); level is cosmetic plus a small passive Cash bonus of +1% per level capped at +25%.
Difficulty ramp, all data-driven from one JSON table:
- Distance targets: S1 D = 1500 + 200*(m-1) → 1,500 → 4,300 m. S2 D = 4000 + 300*(m-16) → 4,000 → 8,200 m. S3 D = 7000 + 500*(m-31) → 7,000 → 14,000 m.
- Target average speed: targetAvg = min(185, 70 + 2.5*m) km/h → m1 72.5, m20 120, m45 182.5.
- Timer: time_limit_s = D / (targetAvg/3.6) * slack, slack = 1.35 - 0.30*(m-1)/44 → 1.35 at m1, 1.05 at m45. From mission 18 onward the raw timer alone cannot reach the target; checkpoints plus close-pass time are required.
- Overtake targets: N = 10 + 1.6*m → 12 at m1, 82 at m45. Near-miss targets: N = 4 + 0.9*m → 5 to 44.
- Traffic density (mean longitudinal gap): S1 lerps 120 m → 55 m, S2 70 m → 38 m, S3 45 m → 25 m. Erratic-driver share 5% → 30% across the career; indicator telegraph 1.2 s → 0.7 s (floor 0.7, never 0).
- recommended_power(m) = 20 + 3.6*m on a 0-200 composite scale, which produces a 'you need a better bike' wall at roughly missions 16 and 32 — deliberately aligned with the two bike unlocks.

**BIKES: 3, gated by price AND career progress (money alone cannot skip the ladder).**
- B1 STREETER 650 (naked): free, owned at first boot. base top speed 180 km/h, accel 45, handling 74, brakes 58, aMax 4.0 m/s², 6 gears, redline 11,500 rpm, inline-twin voicing. Nominal price 8,000 for the upgrade formula.
- B2 KESTREL 900R (sport): 18,000 Cash, requires mission 16. base 250 km/h, accel 68, handling 82, brakes 74, aMax 5.2 m/s², redline 14,000, inline-four voicing.
- B3 VANTA 1200 (hyper): 60,000 Cash, requires mission 32. base 320 km/h, accel 92, handling 88, brakes 90, aMax 6.4 m/s², redline 13,200, big-bore four voicing. Deliberate tradeoff: B3's raw handling number is higher but its maxLateral curve falls off harder above 250 km/h, so B2 remains the better bike for dense two-way traffic — the roster is never a pure straight upgrade until it is fully built.
Buying auto-equips. Upgrades are stored per bike and never inherited.

**UPGRADES: 4 tracks x 5 tiers, one formula.**
cost(bike, tier) = round50(nominalPrice * f[tier]), f = [0.03, 0.05, 0.08, 0.12, 0.18]. Per track that is 46% of the bike's price; all four tracks = 184%, so a fully built bike costs ~2.84x its sticker.
- B1 (nominal 8,000): 250 / 400 / 650 / 950 / 1,450 per track = 3,700/track, 14,800 to max.
- B2 (18,000): 550 / 900 / 1,450 / 2,150 / 3,250 = 8,300/track, 33,200 to max.
- B3 (60,000): 1,800 / 3,000 / 4,800 / 7,200 / 10,800 = 27,600/track, 110,400 to max.
Effects, linear per tier: ENGINE top speed +3%/tier (+15% at 5/5), ACCELERATION aMax +5%/tier (+25%), HANDLING maxLateral and lateral-accel limit +4%/tier (+20%), BRAKES brake decel +4.4%/tier (+22%). Displayed 0-100 bars clamp at 100 but raw values keep going, so a maxed B3 genuinely out-handles a maxed B2 on the physics side. A maxed cheap bike is intentionally weaker than a stock bike one rung up, which teaches buying up.
Buy order is enforced (no skipping tiers, no refunds). A 'recommended' spark badge sits on the cheapest row that most improves earnings — ACCELERATION and HANDLING early, ENGINE last.

**COSMETICS (no stat effect, stated in the panel):** 10 solid paints free per bike, 6 premium finishes at 2,500 / 3,500 / 5,000 Cash unlocking account-wide, 2 animated iridescent at 25 Gold, 6 rim colours free. Rider helmet colour follows the paint choice and is visible on the gloves in first person.

**WORLD UNLOCKS (career mission OR Cash, whichever comes first):** Suburb/Noon free; Suburb/Night at mission 6 or 3,000; Desert at mission 16 or 10,000; weather layer (Rain/Fog) at mission 20 or 15,000; Winter at mission 31 or 25,000; City/Night at mission 38 or 40,000. Clearing a stage grants its environment free in all other modes. Endless unlocks at mission 3, Time Trial at mission 8; Free Ride is available from the first boot.

**DAILY / ACHIEVEMENTS:** 3 daily quests seeded deterministically from the UTC date key, drawn from the objective library with day-scaled targets (ride 15 km total, 60 close passes, 250 overtakes, 30 wheelie seconds, clear 1 career mission), each paying 0.5 * reward_cash(highestUnlocked) plus 2 Gold for the set. 24 achievements across distance / top speed / close-pass count / combo / wheelie / collection families, paying 1-3 Gold and 250-5,000 XP; incremental ones show progress bars.

## Modelo de conducción

**Not a motorcycle simulation. Longitudinal speed integrator + lateral position on a lane strip + a visual lean angle. Fixed timestep 1/120 s with an accumulator, max 4 substeps per rendered frame, because at 300 km/h closing speed a 60 Hz frame advances 1.4 m and discrete collision checks miss.**

ROAD GEOMETRY: +Z forward, +X right, Y up, metres. 4 lanes x 3.6 m = 14.4 m of asphalt. Lane centres at x = -5.4, -1.8, +1.8, +5.4. ONE-WAY: all four lanes travel +Z. TWO-WAY: forward lanes at +1.8 and +5.4, oncoming at -1.8 and -5.4, centreline at x = 0 (double-yellow decal; one-way uses a single white centre line — the mesh is identical, only the marking atlas UVs and traffic direction flags change). Paved shoulder 2.0 m each side (asphalt edge x = ±9.2), barrier face at x = ±9.6, player hard clamp at x = ±8.6. Road streams as 40 m pooled chunks, 16 alive (640 m), 2 retained behind the camera; optional yaw drift ±2 deg per 100 m and grade ±1.5% so the road is not perfectly straight. No chunk boundary may ever be visible inside the fog end plane.

LONGITUDINAL: a = aMax * (1 - (v/vMax)^1.6) when throttle is applied, where aMax is 4.0 / 5.2 / 6.4 m/s² for B1/B2/B3 before upgrades (+5% per ACCELERATION tier) and vMax is the bike's top speed (+3% per ENGINE tier). This gives a mid-tier bike a ~7 s recovery from a hard brake back to 100 km/h. Brake decel 11.0 m/s² dry (+4.4% per BRAKES tier), scaled by the surface grip coefficient: dry 1.00, wet 0.82, snow 0.68, gravel/shoulder 0.75. Engine braking on throttle release = 25% of brake strength (2.75 m/s² dry). Throttle and brake are internally 0..1 with 0.10 s and 0.06 s ramps so audio and camera never snap; Auto-throttle (the default) pins throttle at 1.0 and leaves the player only the brake.

GEARBOX: 6 gears, ratios [2.80, 1.95, 1.55, 1.30, 1.13, 1.00] against a per-bike final drive. rpm = clamp(wheelSpeed * finalDrive * ratio[g], idleRpm, redlineRpm). Auto-upshift at rpm >= 0.90 * redline with throttle > 0.6; auto-downshift at rpm <= 0.42 * redline. Shift gesture 110-160 ms: Engine bus ducks 7 dB with a 15 ms fall and 90 ms recovery, a shift clunk fires (2 variants, pitch 0.96-1.05, 120 ms retrigger cooldown), rpm snaps to prev * ratio[g+1]/ratio[g], the digit punches 1.25x for 120 ms and the RPM ring resets to ~35%. Manual mode: a well-timed shift within 400 rpm of the redline grants +8% acceleration for 1.5 s; over-revving for more than 2 s costs 15% acceleration. Rev limiter engages within 200 rpm of the redline at throttle > 0.8 (11 Hz amplitude gate, pitch frozen), releases 400 rpm below.

LATERAL: target lateral velocity = steerInput * maxLateral(v), integrated under an acceleration limit so direction reversals take time. maxLateral(v) piecewise-linear: 9.0 m/s at 60 km/h, 6.5 at 150, 4.5 at 250, 3.5 at 330 (+4% per HANDLING tier). Lateral acceleration limit 22 m/s², raised to 30 m/s² below 80 km/h — this is the encoded rule that slowing down makes the impossible gap possible, and it is the game's main taught skill. Braking therefore visibly increases manoeuvrability. Barrier contact below 80 km/h is a graze: sparks, 8% speed loss, camera shake, no fail; at or above 80 km/h it is a crash. Visual lean = clamp(lateralVelocity / maxLateral(v), -1, 1) * 32 deg through a 12 Hz critically damped spring with 15% counter-lean overshoot on reversal; the camera takes 0.35x of the lean as roll (full roll is nauseating).

WHEELIE: entry requires kmh >= 70, throttle 1.0, and |lean| < 10 deg, either by button or by the gesture (brief throttle release then re-apply). While up: front wheel rises, camera pitches +7 deg so the near road is hidden behind the fairing (this is the cost), steering authority -45%, a stability meter drains over 8 s and must be managed with micro throttle. Exits on throttle release, brake, |lean| > 18 deg, or meter empty.

STEERING INPUT PIPELINE (shared by all schemes, then per-scheme conditioning): TILT — subtract the calibrated neutral (30 samples averaged over 0.5 s), clamp ±30 deg, deadzone ±2.0 deg with re-normalisation, full lock at 22/sensitivity deg, expo out = sign*|x|^1.3, one-pole low-pass alpha 0.20 per 60 Hz frame; re-zero on resume if the pose differs by > 12 deg, with a toast. BUTTONS — attack 0.12 s, release 0.08 s, auto-centre at 4x the attack rate, left+right = 0, swipe-into-button and 3-finger multitouch supported. HANDLEBAR — full lock at 35 deg of on-screen rotation, returns to centre at 180 deg/s, default sensitivity 1.2. KEYBOARD/GAMEPAD — arrows or WASD steer, Shift/Ctrl or triggers throttle/brake, R/F gears, H horn, Space wheelie, N nitro, Q/E look, C camera, P/Esc pause; analog stick maps through the same expo curve. If no DeviceOrientation event arrives within 1.5 s of enabling tilt (or permission is denied on iOS), auto-fall-back to buttons and say why.

CAMERA RIG: eye at y = 1.35 m, 0.35 m behind the handlebar clamp, pitched 3 deg down. In frame: handlebar with grips and animated levers, two mirrors, the top of the cluster, gloved hands (rotating with lean, squeezing on brake, twisting on throttle) and the tank front. Every channel is a critically damped spring, never a lerp: pitch -1.6 deg at full throttle / +2.8 deg at full brake at 9 Hz; Z offset -3 cm under accel, +5 cm under brake; fork travel 0 → 70 mm compressed, 25 mm extended; roll = 0.35 * lean; yaw ±60 deg over 0.25 s for look-left/right with a deliberate 0.1 s delay; bob and shake per the HUD numbers; FOV 62 → 88 deg. A 'reduced camera motion' toggle scales bob/shake/pitch to 25% and freezes FOV.

TRAFFIC AI: pool of 24 vehicles across 4 GLB classes (sedan, van/SUV, truck, and a re-skinned sedan variant), GPU-instanced. Spawn 320 m ahead for same-direction and 500 m ahead for oncoming (closing speed up to 430 km/h ≈ 4.6 s of warning), despawn 60 m behind. Each vehicle gets a lane, a cruise speed drawn from that lane's N(mean, 8 km/h) clamped to ±2σ, a class, and a personality (docile 4% lane-change probability, normal 12%, erratic 30%). One-way lane means: 60 / 75 / 90 / 105 km/h by lane index; trucks and vans are restricted to lanes 0-1 and capped at 75 km/h; oncoming vehicles cruise 70-100 km/h and oncoming density is capped at 60% of same-direction density. Car-following holds a 1.8 s headway with visible brake lights, which produces the accelerate/brake texture the genre is known for. Lane changes: rolled every 4-12 s, committed only if the target lane is clear 25 m ahead and 15 m behind; indicator blinks at 2.5 Hz for 0.7-1.2 s (an emissive quad with bloom, legible at 200 m — this telegraph is the fairness contract and never goes to zero), then a 1.2-1.8 s eased traverse, indicator held 0.3 s after arrival. A change that would intersect the player within 0.9 s at current closing speed is aborted and the indicator cancelled, which reads as the driver noticing you. AI never actively swerves into the player; the only player-reactive behaviours are the horn rule and a flinch (brake lights + 5% slow) on a pass inside 0.6 m. Spawn rejection rule: at least one lane must be passable within any 60 m window, retried until satisfied.

HORN: yields only when player speed < 100 km/h AND the target is 18-45 m ahead AND an adjacent lane is free. On success the target blinks for 0.7 s then executes a normal lane change out of the player's lane. On failure the sound still plays plus a brake-light flash — never a silent no-op. Cooldown 0.8 s. Deliberately useless at speed: 'I'm going too fast to ask politely' is the designed tension.

COLLISION: player AABB 0.55 m wide x 1.90 m long, 12% narrower than the visible bike so passes that look like contact do not kill. Swept (continuous) tests against vehicles within 25 m longitudinally only. A 60 ms coyote window forgives contact if the steer input in the last 60 ms would have cleared the gap — this single rule removes most 'that was unfair' reports. Vehicle contact at a relative speed above 15 km/h is a crash; below that it is a bump costing 25% speed (strict career missions with the no-crash modifier treat any contact as a crash).

CRASH SEQUENCE (total 1.4-1.8 s, skippable by tap after 600 ms): t=0 hit-stop freezing the simulation for 110 ms, 30% white flash, 400 ms two-pulse haptic, engine hard-killed over 100 ms into an impact plus metal-scrape layer, master lowpass sweeping 22 kHz → 700 Hz over 250 ms and holding 400 ms while master dips 8 dB; t=110 ms timescale drops to 0.25 and ramps back to 1.0 over 0.8 s while the camera detaches into a rigid body, inheriting pre-crash velocity, tumbling at 180-420 deg/s and colliding with the road, motion blur to full and FOV +10 deg; the bike tumbles with 2-3 bounces and the rider plays one of 3 canned tumble clips. Speed reads 0 immediately. Then a 0.5 s freeze-frame and a crossfade to Results. Retry never tears down the scene — pooled traffic resets, the player transform resets, camera view / control scheme / gear mode persist — so crash to controllable again is under 2.0 s.

## Riesgos señalados

- Engine audio is the single biggest make-or-break and the hardest thing here. A fully synthesised RPM x LOAD model can easily land as a buzzing sawtooth rather than a motorcycle. Mitigation: build audio_engine_model as a standalone tunable page (rpm and load sliders, per-harmonic gain, cutoff curves, resonant peak frequency) in week 1 and sign it off before any gameplay work; keep a fallback path that swaps the synth for three 2 s recorded loops per bike (~600 KB) if the synth is rejected, since nothing above the model depends on how it produces sound.
- One 4096x4096 world atlas plus a 2048 UI atlas is a hard constraint that will fight the artist. Every new environment must fit into unused atlas regions or reuse existing ones by tint. Mitigation: freeze the atlas layout as a JSON region map before art starts, forbid per-environment textures by policy, and accept that Rain and Fog are grading plus particle layers over existing surfaces rather than new materials.
- Fog end and the road chunk buffer are load-bearing: if fog end ever exceeds 640 m (through a quality-tier bug, an ultrawide viewport, or a designer typo in the environment table) chunk recycling becomes visible and the illusion collapses instantly. Mitigation: clamp fogEnd to 0.65 * (chunkLength * liveChunks) at load, assert it in dev, and expose it in the debug overlay.
- Close-pass clearance measurement is fragile. Measuring at one frame with a 120 Hz substep and swept colliders can report the wrong tier for a fast diagonal pass, which players read as the scoring being random. Mitigation: track minimum lateral clearance over the whole overlap interval per vehicle, latch one award per vehicle, and log tier decisions behind a debug flag so QA can replay disputed passes.
- The 45-mission timer curve is the most likely thing to be mistuned, and it is invisible until late. If slack plus checkpoint bonuses are slightly generous the last stage is trivial; slightly tight and it is impossible without a maxed bike, which players read as a paywall even though there is no IAP. Mitigation: keep the whole ramp in one JSON table with a headless simulator that plays every mission at three skill profiles (timid / average / expert close-pass rate) and reports pass/fail and star tier, run on every table change.
- Tilt steering on the web is the least reliable input path: iOS requires a user-gesture permission request, some Android browsers deliver no gamma at all, and orientation lock is unavailable on iOS Safari. Mitigation: buttons are the recommended default on touch, tilt auto-falls-back after 1.5 s with no events and explains why, and the rotate-device panel is treated as the real orientation defence rather than lock().
- WebAudio voice count and node graph churn is the hidden CPU cost, especially the 6 spatialised traffic voices plus 5 engine voices plus whooshes on a low-end Android. Symptoms show up as frame stutter, not as audio glitches, so it gets misdiagnosed as a rendering problem. Mitigation: preallocate every node at warmup, never create nodes in the hot path, enforce the per-category caps, and put an audio voice counter in the dev overlay next to the frame timer.
- Reusing one bike GLB for both the first-person cockpit and the garage exterior is a real saving but a real constraint: the cockpit needs high-detail bars, levers and cluster while the garage shot needs a clean exterior, and the same triangle budget must serve both. Mitigation: agree the node/LOD split up front (cockpit nodes are hidden in the garage and vice versa, so the two views never pay for each other), and validate at 88 deg FOV — the widest gameplay framing — not at the default 60.
- Score inflation versus economy tuning will drift apart if anyone adds the combo or speed multiplier to a cash event. Score reaching 8 digits is intended; cash reaching 8 digits would break every bike price in one run. Mitigation: route all cash awards through one function that physically cannot see comboMult or speedMult, and add a test asserting that a 60-second scripted expert run yields cash inside a 100-200 per km band.
- There is no server, so leaderboards are local-only and progress lives in IndexedDB, which private-mode browsers and storage eviction can wipe. Losing a 45-mission career with no recourse is the worst possible outcome for a player. Mitigation: three rotating backup slots plus a localStorage checksum mirror, a visible memory-only warning when storage is blocked, and an export/import save-as-JSON pair in Settings shipped from day one rather than added later.
